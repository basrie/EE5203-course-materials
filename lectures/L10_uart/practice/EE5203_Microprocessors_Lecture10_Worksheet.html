EE5203 L10 Practice Worksheet

Serial communication: UART, I2C and SPI - Basri Erdogan

Expected time: 30-45 minutes

Rule: Try every question before you open the solutions. Short answers are fine. One sentence is usually enough.

In every question: USART2 uses the frame 8N1, so one character is 10 bits on the wire. PCLK1 = 16 MHz. I2C1 runs at 100 kHz unless the question says otherwise. One I2C byte with its ACK takes 9 SCL clocks.
